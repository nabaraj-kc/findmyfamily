# Phase 7: Dashboard & Analytics (Admin)

## Context
As the "Find My Family" platform is adopted during a crisis, official coordinators need a bird's-eye view of the system to allocate resources, monitor data ingestion velocity, and identify bottlenecks (e.g., a backlog in the moderation queue for deceased cases). 

## Core Requirements (REQ-013, REQ-014)
- **High-Level KPIs**: Display total missing persons, total found, reunited cases, and pending verifications.
- **Recent Activity Feed**: A stream of the most recent critical cases entered into the system.
- **Admin Gateway**: This acts as the landing page for officials, linking off to the Matching Review Queue (Phase 6) and other future management tools.

## UI/UX Pro Max Imperatives
- The dashboard must look like a premium SaaS control center. It should use our foundational color palette (slate backgrounds with stark white cards) to establish calm authority.
- The typography and spacing around numbers must be generous to ensure readability at a glance in stressful command-center environments.
